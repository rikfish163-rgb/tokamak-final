LOCKED SPLIT SPECIFICATION
==========================

J-TEXT:
  train/val/test are the three files under splits/. They are used by the
  source-side training code and are kept in chronological shot order.

EAST support:
  config/splits/east_paper131_split_all_in.json
  fixed 110 shots, HDF5-labelled support (10 disruptive / 100 safe).

EAST method-development stream:
  config/splits/east_hdf5_authoritative_balanced360_v1.json
  fixed 360 shots, balanced 180 disruptive / 180 safe, evaluated in shot order.

The 360-shot stream is a locked-best method-development/diagnostic stream. It
was available during earlier method development and is not an untouched future
confirmation set. HDF5 meta/IsDisrupt and meta/DownTime override any curated
CSV/JSON outcome label. Any new split must receive a new versioned manifest and
must not overwrite these files.
