## Defensible estimator

**Prefer density-only gain as the small-budget primary candidate; retain identity and multichannel moments as controls.** The [existing estimator](/home/dianrobot/Fuison-final/code/research/east360-online-adaptation/experiments/risk_aligned_online/warmstart_alignment_20261022/alignment.py:36) assigns equal total weight per observed discharge/channel and caps observations at 128. Five discharges remain five correlated experimental units, not 640 independent examples.

For a general affine control, declare source/target reference definitions and use `a=s_source/s_target`, `b=c_source−a*c_target`. Apply it **before** the frozen network's `asinh((x−center)/scale)`; do not replace the trained preprocessor with z-score. Reference mean/std and checkpoint median/IQR are distinct objects, including different sampling/validity conventions. Preserve all normalization buffers, availability lanes, embedding, and head. [Actual normalization](/home/dianrobot/Fuison-final/code/zero_cold_start/backbone.py:44).

Use only signal-defined historical sampling, no target DownTime crop, class selection, AdvancedTime, or future-stream summaries. Full donor recordings become available after their shots end. Keep identity for missing/unidentifiable spread and distinguish constant signals from sparse zero-IQR activity. Broad transport may erase true regime differences or source class geometry; call it **statistical**, not physical calibration.

A stronger prior needs provenance from actual magnet/geometry specifications or forward-model diagnostics to these exact processed channels, intervals, units, signs, and scaler coordinates. Typical machine parameters do not determine feature mean/std. No authenticated local device-prior bundle was found in the inspected files.
