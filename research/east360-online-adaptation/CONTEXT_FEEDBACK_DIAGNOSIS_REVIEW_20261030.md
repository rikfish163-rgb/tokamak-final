Interpretation checks:

- **The mixed AUC/log-loss result matters.** Combined real feedback exceeds TRAIN-mean AUC by only 0.002164578, while log loss is worse by 0.111030913. The mean arm retains AUC 0.907613 versus origin-only 0.891088: that difference cannot be assigned to instance-specific arrival feedback, because the combined probe has different TRAIN-fitted origin coefficients. Reversal hurts both metrics, showing sensitivity of this readout to block ordering, not proof of physical temporal information or online benefit. Controls also change availability features and can move inputs off their training distribution.
- **Probe generalization is limited.** Saved-coefficient TRAIN log loss is origin 0.126582436, feedback 0.454224624, combined 0.064848218, maturity 0.101673075, versus the substantially larger VAL values above. Fixed C and differing feature counts do not establish which representation contains more information in general. No uncertainty estimate or significance claim is made.
- **The frozen risk head is a relevant reference.** Frozen maturity-logit AUC/log loss (0.948857/0.450767) outperform the maturity linear probe (0.921128/0.772748). These are distinct readouts, and maturity also has a later observation window. The query-logit reference is explicitly future, at a parent-selected peak, and cannot be used as an online decision available at origin or maturity. [Collection](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/source_diagnose.py:105); [future flag](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/source_diagnose.py:166); [query selection](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/source_episodes.py:95).
- **Probe group deltas use a different reference.** Their `before_bce` is ln(2), from zero logits; `delta_bce` and improved/worsened are relative to that constant, not the frozen source. Actual adaptation before/after is in `train_fit.json`/`val_fit.json`. The implementation is consistent; interpreting probe deltas as controller improvements would be wrong. [Zero reference](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/source_diagnose.py:165).
