# EAST intra-shot forecast/adaptation history — bounded audit, 2026-10-31

## Bottom line

The combination “fit a J-TEXT diagnostic forecast head on an ended EAST prefix, then use arrived H25 forecast errors to update first-layer Q/K” **has already been executed**. In that specific 360-shot development stream, the prefix-fitted head did not pass the predeclared intra-shot screen: it produced 127 TP/34 FP versus 125/32 without intra-shot updates, and its reversed-feedback control produced the same 127/34. Its next-window forecast error improved relative to the uncalibrated head but remained well above persistence. This is not evidence that every possible calibration or internal update must fail.

The main agent's proposed **H0 versus H25 conditional-capacity readout is also a close duplicate of an executed probe**: `readout_identifiability_v2` already fitted current, absolute-future and persistence-skip incremental targets from the same fixed cold hidden views. It used five 16-train/4-held shot folds, not leave-one-shot-out, and it did not evaluate the later 340 shots. The exact comparison and its limits are below. The previously drafted affine-head candidate is withdrawn from this report: the main agent decided not to repeat head calibration. This was a research decision, not a new user instruction.

Six closest executed experiment families are below. A separate mean-anchored Q/K experiment also ran in full stream, but **its anchor is a learned representation target, not the source H25 forecast residual**. An older per-channel forecast-bias correction gave a real but fragile one-shot improvement; it is not a new proposal either.

## Focused answer: H0 versus H25 conditional-capacity probe

**Verdict: yes, the core matched-readout question was already executed; no, the exact requested 19/1 split and suffix340 audit were not.** The executed [completion](probe_outputs/readout_identifiability_v2/complete.json) explicitly says `complete=true` and `test_or_suffix_access=false`. Its [saved results](probe_outputs/readout_identifiability_v2/results.json), [protocol](probe_outputs/readout_identifiability_v2/protocol.json), [ridge/target implementation](experiments/risk_aligned_online/readout_identifiability_20261029/learning.py), and [fold runner](experiments/risk_aligned_online/readout_identifiability_20261029/run.py) establish the actual run rather than merely the prior plan. The frozen source SHA and `lifecycle_context8360_service_20261028` cold operator/map match the parent in item 1.

- **Exact overlap.** At each fixed causal clock, the same per-channel, equal-shot-weighted ridge fit and `0.01` penalty jointly predict `current`, future absolute H25, and `future - current`; the last output is evaluated after adding the **observed** last value back. This is exactly a persistence-skip increment output, not an autoregressive prediction. Fixed hidden views were all 17×64 final tokens and the final 64-dimensional signal token; the third comparator was raw recent8 with masks. There was no encoder, risk-head or online adaptation update.
- **Data and clock.** EAST cold20: 20 shots, 275 windows, clocks `256, 384, ...`, at most 16 per shot. Five deterministic folds each fit 16 whole shots and hold out 4; a separate fit using all20 was saved as [cold_readouts.npz](EXTERNAL_ASSETS.md). Thus changing to leave-one-shot-out would be a different split/sample count, **not a new H0/H25 mechanism**. The saved all20 fit was in-sample on those 20 and was not evaluated on suffix340.
- **Actual cold20 held-fold numbers** (shot-averaged masked SmoothL1, not risk metrics): all-token H0 **0.030393**, direct H25 **0.050958**, skip-increment H25 **0.049136**; last-token H0 **0.019495**, direct H25 **0.041954**, skip-increment H25 **0.034219**; persistence H25 **0.026810**. Raw8 H0 **0.003053**. Last-token skip beat persistence on only **5/20** shots. H0 and H25 targets have different temporal dimensions, so their raw losses are not a standalone measure of conditional information or proof of absent dynamics.
- **Mask and leakage caveats.** The same channel mask requires the last 8 points, an additional prior point for trend, and **all 25 future points** to be available; `mode_number_n` is excluded, EAST MNM is unavailable, and CIII is unestimable when its only supporting shot is held out. Thus this is not an unconditional 23-channel reconstruction audit. The fixed cold operator and density map were themselves fitted using all 20 shots; applying the map to those same cold20 features is retrospective and differs from their original source-only first-20 online predictions. Even 19/1 readout fitting would remain conditional on that shared cold parent, not clean full-pipeline leave-one-shot-out. Clocks were selected retrospectively using available record length; outcomes were not used and the scored windows were not censored against disruption time, so they are not all established preterminal windows.
- **What would actually be added.** The intended **suffix340 assessment** has not been executed in this saved probe, and leave-one-shot-out changes the conditional fold. A paired source-only-versus-cold-operator representation comparison on the **same mapped EAST shots** is likewise not established here: source-only readouts were fitted/evaluated on J-TEXT TRAIN/VAL, whereas the EAST readout used the cold operator. A CLS-only readout is not one of the named standalone views (CLS is included in the all-token view). Any such extra comparison must be described by its actual scope, not advertised as the first current-versus-future probe or as online alarm gain.

The earlier [independent audit](probe_outputs/readout_identifiability_v2/audit_with_fold_gap.json) reports 20 EAST shots, zero outcome or suffix reads, independent NumPy cold coefficient maximum error `3.69e-13` and OOF prediction maximum error `6.98e-12`; it reused the production neural feature forward. This bounded audit independently checked the saved results/fit-fold counts and verified the results and prediction-file hashes against completion, but did not rerun the fit or feature extraction. The previous forecast-head calibration at roughly 9.95× persistence is item 1 and is not a reason to rerun it.

## 1. Source and ended-cold20 forecast heads driving Q/K — executed, full 360

**Artifacts and code.** [Complete results](probe_outputs/qk_forecast_east360_v1/complete.json), [protocol](probe_outputs/qk_forecast_east360_v1/protocol.json), [cold-head training receipt](probe_outputs/qk_forecast_east360_v1/cold_head_training.json), [next-window diagnostic](probe_outputs/qk_forecast_east360_v1/prequential.json), [run-time learning code](experiments/risk_aligned_online/qk_forecast_stream_20261029/learning.py). The source-trained auxiliary head was the forecast-only output of [qk_matched_source_v1](probe_outputs/qk_matched_source_v1/complete.json), SHA `94442cb888068289a598e57c2318c127cdad3f93fc6a5ae33bf76b91605a9867`; it is a head copy trained on J-TEXT, **not** a claim that the exact original qualified H25 head was unchanged in every arm. The separate risk-meta head in that source run failed its source screen and was not deployed here.

**Parameters, target and clock.** With classifier, risk head, cold operator, threshold and map fixed, the `source_forecast` arm uses that source-only forecast head. After the first 20 EAST shots were sealed and ended, `cold_forecast` starts from it and fits **only the forecast-head parameters**, 256 AdamW steps, learning rate `1e-4`, batch four ended shots, seed 131, using actual arrived diagnostic values and masks; no current-shot labels. The source classifier file remains untouched. Each subsequent feedback step instead computes a fresh disposable **8,192-coordinate first-layer Q/K** gradient from masked H25 SmoothL1, normalized to step norm 0.05. Four nonoverlapping arrived H25 targets use W128 inputs; feedback begins at 256 observed points and recurs every 128. Each proposal resets to the fixed cold state; the whole shot is discarded afterward; no post-cold20 persistent update. Reversal and hold are controls on the same heads and clock, not independent safety guarantees.

**Observed result.** Full360 TP/FP: cold **125/32**; original-head Q/K **126/33**; source forecast **126/32**; source reverse and source hold **126/32 each**; cold20-fitted forecast **127/34**; cold reverse **127/34**; cold hold **127/33**. Source real/reverse/hold all rescued shot 85023; the prefix-fitted real and reverse each rescued 74970 and 85023 and added false alarms 64562 and 87847. Both source and cold candidates' saved screens are false. There were 10,774 update opportunities per adaptive arm.

The retrospective next-window diagnostic sampled 1,352 eligible clocks on the same suffix340. Shot-mean SmoothL1 was source head **0.592989 → 0.586937** after its Q/K step, cold20-fitted head **0.252509 → 0.248885**, versus hold-last **0.025006**. Each head improved versus itself on all 340 shots, but neither beat hold on any shot. These are retrospective predictive losses, not alarm rates or independent validation.

**Interpretation.** The measured rescue is not specific to the true source-head target because reverse/hold rescue the same shot; the cold-head fit reduces measured forecast error without improving full-stream J or beating its controls. It is plausible that common forecast bias or weak gradient contrast dominates, but the controls and aggregate error do not by themselves identify the cause. Repeating “fit the same head after cold20 + residual-to-Q/K” as a new idea would be a duplicate.

## 2. Mean-anchored representation feedback into Q/K — executed, full 360

**Artifacts and code.** [Full-stream completion](probe_outputs/anchored_full360_diagnostic_v1/complete.json), [protocol](probe_outputs/anchored_full360_diagnostic_v1/protocol.json), [anchored controller](experiments/risk_aligned_online/anchored_feedback_20261030/learning.py), [gradient construction](experiments/risk_aligned_online/anchored_feedback_20261030/run.py), [stream clock](experiments/risk_aligned_online/anchored_stream_20261031/stream.py), and [independent component audit](ANCHORED_FEEDBACK_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT_20261030.md). Same cold20 parent/order as item 1.

**Parameters, target and clock.** A 14,316-parameter auxiliary target network was fitted from all 20 ended shots' historical **risk labels**, 256 Adam steps, batch 24, learning rate 0.001, seed 131, for each of origin and mature-gradient variants. It produces `0.5 * [f(origin, arrived_feedback) - f(origin, fit_mean_feedback)]`; a representation squared loss differentiates to disposable first-layer Q/K, with step 0.1 and joint radius 0.1. The signal-feedback action itself uses only arrived observations; the offline controller fitting is supervised, **not** self-supervised H25-head prediction. Original source, cold slow state, risk head and threshold remain fixed. Origin 127, first matured feedback 152, then every 25 points; score at maturity is sealed first and the new state affects maturity+1 onward. The action is recomputed from zero at each feedback, not accumulated.

**Observed result.** Full360: cold **125/32**, origin-real **127/37**, origin-reverse **128/36**, origin-donor **125/36**, mature-real **126/36**, mature-reverse **127/37**, mature-donor **126/37**. Mean-feedback is exactly zero by construction and equals cold, so it is not a substantive proof of feedback benefit. Origin-real exceeds the 20% FPR cap (37/180); both real variants have J **0.5000** versus cold **0.5167**, and neither meets the saved operational or control gates. The run has 55,565 applied updates per nonzero arm; both variants had already failed the component screen and this full stream was an explicitly disclosed supplementary diagnostic, not promotion.

**Interpretation.** The additional false alarms and reverse comparisons are observed; “anchoring did not remove all origin×feedback interactions” is a code-level limitation, not proof of why each alarm changed. This is a close anchored-Q/K precedent, but it does not establish that a source-H25-residual-anchored target was tested. Its labels used for controller fitting must not be described as unlabeled online learning.

## 3. Separately trained expected dynamics and residual memory — source risk screen; EAST forecast only

**Artifacts and code.** [Paired source completion](probe_outputs/expected_residual_pair_v1/complete.json), [paired protocol](probe_outputs/expected_residual_pair_v1/protocol.json), [EAST forecast-only completion](probe_outputs/expected_forecast_east360_v1/complete.json), [memory code](experiments/risk_aligned_online/expected_dynamics_20261028/memory.py), [pair runner](experiments/risk_aligned_online/expected_dynamics_20261028/pair.py), and the [historical results review](references/预期演化_源域配对与EAST跨域失效_20261028.md). The source-screen reference was `context8_scaled_source_service_20261028`; this is not an EAST Q/K stream.

**Parameters, target and clock.** A separate frozen 92→64→92 predictor (11,932 parameters, not the original H25 head) was source-trained on 1,349 eligible J-TEXT training shots to predict changes in the next four **8-point patches**. The paired source experiment trained 12,889 memory parameters (`key`, `kernel`, `rate`, `output`) for two epochs with source risk and auxiliary forecast losses. The already-issued predicted change was subtracted from the corresponding matured real change to write an 8-dimensional shot-local state injected between Transformer blocks. The source classifier and expectation predictor remained frozen. Feedback is available only when each future patch has matured; the four target horizons are 8/16/24/32 samples, not H25. The EAST360 follow-up evaluated the frozen forecaster only: its completion records zero optimizer steps and no risk predictions.

**Observed result.** On source VAL160, the frozen predictor's weighted MSE was **0.021686** versus persistence **0.025305** (14.3% lower). In the paired source risk screen, disruptive bag loss was **0.370602** with residual memory versus **0.366583** off; the saved gate was ineligible and no EAST risk-update result followed. On EAST360, the fixed forecast MSE was **0.046412** versus persistence **0.029025**, 59.9% worse; only **1/360** shots improved. A historical level-response decomposition was retrospective, not an implemented calibration.

**Interpretation.** Predictive transfer and source risk gating both failed in the measured senses; this does not prove that all residual state feedback is useless. Source-level extrapolation is a plausible contributor in the saved decomposition, not an identified physical or unique cause.

## 4. Disposable input affine, mean-preserving gain and source-risk weighting — older EAST120

**Artifacts and code.** Exact paired summaries: [reversible affine](EXTERNAL_ASSETS.md), [window gain](EXTERNAL_ASSETS.md), [source-weighted affine](EXTERNAL_ASSETS.md), and [source-weight validation](EXTERNAL_ASSETS.md). Their run protocols and `code_snapshot.zip` files are in those same directories; the [current feature-affine code](references/feature_affine.py) is useful for orientation, but is **not byte-identical to the earlier reversible-affine run**. For that run, archived `feature_affine.py` SHA `e993438bf1204f9c9bf7f59d8efea71e55668998db83bed70b3f11a2c61b3cc1` and `density_online.py` SHA `21f391cc9f3e23f85f62ca13d02a7299ca639eccb804e87e65c96671b67d4924` match its saved protocol; current files differ. The window-gain and weighted snapshots have their own recorded versions.

**Parameters, target and clock.** This older ascending-ID prefix has **39 disruptive/81 safe**, includes the first 30 shots before a density mapping fitted on their ended signals, and is not the cold20/360 order. Free reversible affine updates **23 gains + 23 biases** before the frozen encoder; the observed-coordinate forecast is inverse transformed before SmoothL1 against the actual matured H25 target. Window gain instead updates **23 gains around the causal W128 observed mean**, with no temporary offset, preserving that mean only in source-asinh coordinates, not guaranteeing physical or risk preservation. Both use disposable shot-local parameters, a proposed step 0.05, radius 1, one origin every 100 points, with an origin's target maturing at +25 and only +26 onward affected. Source, classifier and risk head are frozen; any separate inter-shot arms use outcome-revealed persistent affine updates and cannot be credited to pure intra.

The third arm additionally source-trained **23 positive loss weights**, using 256 Adam steps on J-TEXT with supervised source risk BCE at origin+26 as the outer objective through the 46-coordinate signal step; its online target is still masked real H25 signal, not EAST risk labels. Source VAL sampled-window BCE was **0.858703** before, **0.859649** after equal-weight feedback, **0.854400** after learned-weight feedback. This is risk-aware source teaching, not a target-domain safety guarantee.

**Observed result.** On the same old 120, density-mapped static **20 TP/8 FP**; reversible affine intra **28/18** (8 rescues and 10 new FPs, FPR 18/81 = 22.22%, above 20%); mean-preserving gain intra **20/8** after 4,514 accepted updates; source-risk-weighted affine intra **20/8**, with a rescue and a lost existing TP and one FP exchange. The respective intra+inter results were 28/16, 20/5 and 20/7; they are not independent intra improvements.

**Interpretation.** Preserving the observed mean removed the measured extra false alarms **and** all eight net extra detections; learning global source risk weights also gave no net intra benefit. It is unsupported to infer from this alone that absolute level shifts are inherently nuisance, physically calibrated, or causally responsible for each error. A plain per-shot input affine, inverse forecast mapping, or window-mean-preserving gain is already executed.

## 5. Older per-channel forecast-bias subtraction — executed, older TEST360

**Artifacts and code.** [Completed result](references/completed.json), [protocol](references/protocol.json), [sensitivity receipt](references/rescued_case.json), [learning code](experiments/risk_aligned_online/lag_bias_feedback_20261022/learning.py). Reference `head_operator_test360_20261022`, density map `deepcoral_prefix_test360_v2_20261022/density_map.json`; its initial 20 are **2 disruptive/18 safe**, not the 17/3 current parent.

**Parameters, target and clock.** No persistent training: from the source H25 forecast residual on the two older completed segments, fit/detach one mean residual per eligible channel; subtract it from prediction-minus-observation on the two newer completed segments, then take a SmoothL1 step on the latter. Risk input is not centered or bias-corrected. The disposable parameters are 16 named tensors covering input projection, CLS/position and first encoder block (listed in the saved protocol), **not just Q/K**; SGD 0.001, gradient norm cap 1, state retained within the shot and discarded after it. W128/H25: after points 0–255 have arrived, old targets are 156–205, new targets 206–255, and the first affected prediction is index 256; repeat every 128. Current-shot outcomes enter only after full prediction sealing.

**Observed result.** Complete old 360: static **118/27**, matched recent-uncorrected **117/29**, lag-bias **119/27**. The sole gain versus static is shot **73405**, first alarm **27 ms → 31 ms**, so it crosses the strict >30 ms boundary by 1 ms; at strict >31 ms the reported gain disappears. Both adaptive arms made 10,635 signal-loss-decreasing steps. The prior report records replay, but this audit did not redo it.

**Interpretation.** This is a real narrow positive on its saved development stream, not a generic failure and not independent confirmation. Its boundary sensitivity and possible removal of a sustained dangerous residual limit the claim. “Estimate old residual bias, detach it, and update on newer H25 feedback” is a duplicate; adding a different label to it would not make it new.

## 6. Frozen conditional readouts and subsequent per-shot latent readout/Q/K — executed

The cold20 `readout_identifiability_v2` experiment described in the focused answer is the **same readout family** and produced the source/cold prior later used by this full-stream follow-up. Its H0/H25 findings must not be conflated with the follow-up's adapted 360-shot risk decisions.

**Artifacts and code.** [Factorial complete result](probe_outputs/local_factorial_east360_v1/complete.json), [protocol](probe_outputs/local_factorial_east360_v1/protocol.json), [saved auxiliary summary](probe_outputs/local_factorial_east360_v1/auxiliary_summary.json), [per-shot ridge fit](experiments/risk_aligned_online/local_readout_20261029/learning.py), [factorial feedback](experiments/risk_aligned_online/local_factorial_20261029/learning.py), and [historical detailed review](references/炮内拟合与反馈分离_11组完整流及步长检验_20261029.md). Same current cold20 parent/order as items 1–2.

**Parameters, target and clock.** After cold20, a per-channel linear readout from 64 last-token features plus intercept to 25 future **increments** is initialized from an ended-cold20 ridge prior. At most 16 older matured rows per shot update its coefficients via a fixed ridge solve; no backpropagation through the solve. It is **not the original H25 head** and cannot directly modify risk. At clock 256 and every 128, older two completed H25 targets enter the readout history; the two newer, disjoint targets drive a disposable 8,192-coordinate Q/K gradient; only afterward do those newer targets join the next history. A bounded Armijo search tests factors of the normalized maximum-0.05 proposal on already observed auxiliary loss. Readout and Q/K reset per shot; Q/K resets to cold each feedback. Risk head, threshold, source and post-cold20 inter state stay fixed.

**Observed result.** On full360, cold **125/32**, fixed prior/real **125/32**, real-history/real-feedback **125/33**, real-history/reversed-feedback **125/33**. Each of the ten update arms has 10,774 accepted updates; the true/true arm's observed auxiliary loss decreased on all of them, yet it added false alarm 64562 and rescued no shot. On 1,352 retrospective next-window samples, true/true loss was **0.034635 → 0.034345**; reverse feedback **0.034331**; hold-last **0.025006**. Its saved intra screen failed.

**Interpretation.** A disjoint same-shot calibration/gradient split and a reliable decrease of arrived feedback loss were already tested, but with a new latent increment readout, not a channelwise affine calibration of the original source H25 prediction. Forecast improvement is not evidence of better first-alarm decisions, and the matched reverse result does not isolate a successful real-feedback mechanism.

## Decision for the proposed bounded probe

Do not repeat the existing current/absolute/skip readout as if its basic question were unexplored. If the main investigation proceeds with the specified **leave-one-shot-out cold20 fit and later suffix340 audit**, label it an extension of `readout_identifiability_v2`, preserve the already completed five-fold result, and report the changed split, identical/changed hidden views, map, masks, time selection and outcome-access rules. Neither a successful reconstruction nor a predictive readout is an online adaptation result. Existing `cold_readouts.npz` contains all20 fitted coefficients; [cold_oof_predictions.npz](EXTERNAL_ASSETS.md) contains targets, masks and predictions, not the hidden feature matrix, so a fresh 19/1 fit would need those features to be obtained under an authorized, otherwise read-only workflow. No new probe was run here.
