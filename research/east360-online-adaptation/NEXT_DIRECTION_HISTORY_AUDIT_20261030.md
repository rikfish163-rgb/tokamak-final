## Current reference

The saved 360-shot result has cold **125 TP/32 FP**, real **126/32**, mean **126/32**, and reverse **126/32**, with denominators 180 disruptive/180 safe. The real and mean arms rescue 74970 by moving its first lead from 29 to 31 ms; reverse instead rescues 85023 (16 to 46 ms). This is a development comparison, not independent confirmation. [Complete result][current-result]; [per-shot decisions][current-shots].

The arrival evidence is a scalar `e`; more precisely, the Q/K step is `-0.05 * (sigmoid(z) - sigmoid(z+e)) * grad_QK(z)/||grad_QK(z)||`, or zero with no support/zero gradient. Thus arrival changes the magnitude/sign along the origin-risk-gradient axis, not an independently learned vector direction. [Evidence calculation][arrival-code]; [proposal][proposal-code].

## One bounded recommendation: context-conditioned representation-target pilot

Use a **single pilot**, not another sweep of transport matrices or score corrections. Keep the qualified J-TEXT parent, original risk head, mapping/threshold contract and archived cold20 predictions fixed for any eventual current-stream comparison.

1. Offline, using ended historical shots only, construct a small set of safe/disruptive **representation prototypes** with equal-shot weighting; disruptive examples may include eligible early history and must not be treated as point-label-0 errors simply for being earlier than 125 ms. Train a small vector-valued rule `v_phi(c, prototypes)` from a causal origin representation plus its actually matured H25 observations/masks. Prototypes provide context, but do **not** constrain the vector to a fixed global span. No current-shot outcome, future label, down-time or event family enters `c`.
2. At maturity, set `target = h_origin_detached + v_phi(c, prototypes)` and take a bounded temporary Q/K step on `||h_theta(origin) - target||²`. Hold the target fixed **with respect to the inner Q/K derivative**; retain the rule's outer-training gradient through that step. The unchanged risk head reads the resulting network; do not add `v`, `e`, a prototype score, or a pseudo-label to its output. Reset at each shot and affect only future observations.
3. Use existing threshold-centered shot utility as an **offline outer surrogate**, not a claimed new objective: safe shots have an all-valid-window peak, disruptive shots a peak over *all* eligible lead>30 ms times. Preserve the actual first-crossing metric and separately report >3000 ms alarms. Compare to point-BCE supervision only as a matched diagnostic; record the fraction of useful/zero pre-alarm training credit. The hard-max sparsity and REINFORCE no-contrast failures above are explicit stop risks, not reasons to infer success.
4. Before any full stream, predeclare held-shot splits and controls: no update; current scalar-arrival reference; a matched-budget scalar learned-loss controller; fixed/context-averaged vector target; same-origin shuffled or time-reversed matured context; and the old arrived-teacher target. Match temporary Q/K norm and also report origin-logit effect so “different direction” is not just a larger step. For the **same origin**, measure cosine and the component orthogonal to its normalized origin-risk gradient; require context changes to alter direction, then assess later shot-level TP/FP flips and both-class harm. A nonzero orthogonal component alone is not alarm benefit. Prespecify an operational gate requiring FPR≤0.20, an improvement over cold and a paired advantage over the context controls; retain all favorable and unfavorable flips.
5. For a conditional-parent screen, explicitly disclose that the current cold model already used all 20 shots. A genuinely held-shot screen must refit every target-side learned map/parent/prototype/rule on fold-fit shots only while keeping the J-TEXT source fixed; the current 17D/3S cold20 supports at most three folds with a safe shot in each holdout and only two safe shots per fit. If this cannot supply credible both-class contrasts, stop and report the limitation. Freeze the rule and controls before any suffix scoring; a later full360 remains a reused development stream, not independent confirmation.

This is a proposed *combination and discriminating test*, not a claim that prototype targets, conditional losses, first-alarm utility, or hypernetworks are novel or effective. It addresses a different internal update direction while directly exposing the already-observed teacher, control, sparse-credit and safety failure modes.
