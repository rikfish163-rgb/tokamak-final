## Decision in brief

**The same-pair forward-versus-reverse direction is mathematically predetermined, as is its finite-step margin improvement when both window caps remain inactive; neither identifies an event-conditioned or useful risk update.** At a common state, the gradients of `softplus(-m)` and `softplus(+m)` are antiparallel for *any differentiable scalar margin* `m`, not just for an affine FFN. At zero state, the proposed first-FFN update is even more constrained: its `4×64` direction has matrix rank at most two and its right-hand directions come from the fixed teacher vector. Normalization removes the order-confidence magnitude.

This does **not** prove the actual trained teacher is random, that directions are identical across different pairs, or that the update cannot improve downstream risk. Pair-dependent projected token differences still matter. The identifiable scientific question is narrower: does this particular TRAIN-fitted, event-selected teacher yield better **held-out, actual risk** than matched-other, shuffled, and arbitrary-teacher directions? The matched-other construction also uses interval annotations; it is not an event-free control. If the risk comparison fails, do not infer an event mapping from low order loss or improved same-pair margin. Main agent owns the decision and any real-data or GPU work.

## 2. Exact gradient and low-rank result

For the same chronological pair and state `W`, write `m(W) = wᵀ S⁻¹[z_later(W) − z_earlier(W)]`, with fixed positive diagonal `S` and fixed teacher `w`. For `L+ = softplus(-m)` and `L− = softplus(+m)`,

```
g+ = ∇W L+ = -sigmoid(-m) ∇W m
g− = ∇W L− =  sigmoid( m) ∇W m = -exp(m) g+ .
```

For finite `m` and nonzero `∇W m`, the gradients are therefore exactly opposite up to a positive scalar, **even if** the cap is active or subsequent representation calculations are nonlinear. One nonzero normalized step from the same state makes `W− = -W+` when starting at zero. This is an algebraic reverse-direction control, not independent evidence that chronological supervision was learned. If `∇W m = 0`, both exact gradients are zero and cosine/normalization is undefined. Floating-point sigmoid saturation, underflow, finite-value checks, or a denominator epsilon can produce additional numerical exceptions; report them. The result does not assert that actual directions are independent of input values: `∇W m` generally depends on the pair.

It also does **not** automatically extend to a loss aggregated across pairs: the sigmoid weight varies by pair. In the executed two-pair synthetic counterexample with `m=(1+W₁, -1+W₂)` at `W=0`, forward and reverse batch gradients were `(-0.134471,-0.365529)` and `(0.365529,0.134471)`; cosine was **−0.648054**, not −1. Reverse waveform content, a changed teacher, or a different state likewise is not the same-pair identity.

For the specified mean/last pooling, let `a_t ∈ R⁴` be the mean valid `phi` and `b_t ∈ R⁴` the last valid `phi`. Write `u_m,u_l ∈ R⁶⁴` for the two halves of `S⁻¹w`. With nonempty masks, `phi` fixed upstream, and the cap inactive at the evaluated state,

```
K = ∇W m = (a_later − a_earlier) ⊗ u_m
           + (b_later − b_earlier) ⊗ u_l .
```

Thus `rank(K) ≤ 2`, and the right/output-side subspace is contained in `span(u_m,u_l)`. The left factors are pair-dependent. This formula applies at `W=0`, where the cap is locally inactive; it is not a rank-two guarantee for gradients at an arbitrary capped state or for a multi-pair aggregate. For a nonzero `K`, the planned update is exactly `W+ = 0.1 K/||K||F` at zero, apart from floating-point/epsilon handling. Positive rescaling of the fixed teacher `w → c w`, `c>0`, leaves that normalized step unchanged; `w → -w` exchanges the two step signs. Neither holds as an unconditional statement about retraining with AdamW. In particular, arbitrarily small *nonzero* raw gradients can still receive a full step under exact unit normalization: log raw norms, floor use, zero/underflow counts, and numerical stability.

## 3. Concrete identifiability and self-fulfilment counterexamples

**Same loss, different update.** Let all TRAIN standardized differences be `d=e₁` in the mean-feature block, with no last-block difference, and let `Δa=e₁` and `Δb=0`. Teachers `w₁=e₁` and `w₂=e₁+10e₂` have exactly the same TRAIN margin `wᵀd=1`, hence the same unregularized order data loss, but their update directions are proportional to `e₁⊗e₁` and `e₁⊗(e₁+10e₂)`. Their cosine is `1/sqrt(101)=0.099504`; increasing 10 makes them arbitrarily close to orthogonal. A CPU calculation confirmed those numbers. This is an admissible synthetic counterexample, not a measurement of actual TRAIN rank. More generally, any teacher perturbation invisible to all TRAIN differences can change `∇W m` if it is not invisible to the insertion Jacobian. Weight decay, initialization and the fixed AdamW schedule may select a particular vector and penalize the second teacher; this example establishes **data-objective non-identifiability**, not that the prescribed optimizer returns either vector or that actual TRAIN has a nullspace.

**A generic drift solves the auxiliary problem.** If the frozen representation contains `z(t)=c t e₁` for a nuisance ramp, then all 25-step chronological differences have the same direction in both event-selected and matched-other pairs. Choosing `w` along `e₁` orders both perfectly, without learning event membership or a risk direction. The bias-free loss without regularization has an infimum as the weight is scaled; the actual AdamW/finite-step procedure need not diverge. The 128-point windows in a pair already share 103 samples; the matched comparator origin is at most 50 samples away, so corresponding windows share at least 78/128 samples. Thus high order accuracy may reflect drift, availability, or sliding-window structure. Conversely, high held-VAL order accuracy before updating is still an empirical result, not a tautology: it just does not establish event-specific risk utility.

**The same-pair update improves its own score by construction.** If both pair windows remain uncapped at the finite `W+`, the margin is affine, so

```
m(W+) − m(0) = +0.1 ||K||F;    m(W−) − m(0) = −0.1 ||K||F.
```

Therefore showing the same pair's order loss falling, or its margin rising, is not independent evidence of an event mapping. If the cap activates, the exact finite-step equality may fail; the same-start reverse-gradient identity above still holds where differentiable. With downstream query logit `q`, the first-order risk change is `0.1 <∇W q,K>/||K||F`, whose sign is unconstrained by the order loss. A toy fixed readout `q(W)=<K,W>` made the exact same positive order step raise the query logit; at baseline `q=0`, safe-label BCE increased by `+0.027192` while danger-label BCE decreased by `−0.026472`. This is a counterexample, not a reported source-model effect. Frozen risk-head weights and the absence of a final-score patch do not create the missing alignment.

## 4. Actual checks performed

I ran three in-memory CPU checks in `/home/hetaisheng/miniconda3/envs/rl_env/bin/python` with `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=''` and `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`. ## 5. Minimal falsifiable preflight for the main agent

This adds a diagnostic control to the existing proposal; it is **not** a completed result or authorization for a new online branch. Fix the control seeds, common standardization, numerical tolerances and decision rule before opening validation risk outcomes. Use the current implementation and existing fixed archive; do not duplicate the framework or tune against VAL.

1. **Path and algebra certificate.** On predeclared examples and both planned mask views, check zero-state source parity, frozen source/slow/head/teacher hashes, valid mean/last pooling, cache/direct FFN insertion parity, causal prefix invariance, and one fresh state per episode. Compare analytic `K`, autograd, forward/reverse cosine and norms; report input-window and query cap fractions, zero/nonfinite/near-underflow gradients and floor counts. Test `2w` and `-w`: their same-pair normalized states should equal true and reverse, respectively, within declared dtype tolerance when nonzero. This is an implementation check, not a performance test. If any required parity or causal check fails, stop.
2. **Arbitrary-readout control.** In addition to the already planned no-update, reverse, whole-event shuffled-order and matched-other teachers, predeclare a few fixed-seed random bias-free `128→1` teacher vectors, normalized to the fitted teacher's weight norm and passed through the **same** frozen TRAIN scale. Do not train, select, or tune them on VAL. For every same origin pair compute their one-step states and actual later frozen-risk logits at the same preselected query, with the same step, cap, masks and zero-state baseline. Record true-vs-random and true-vs-matched-other state cosines, finite query logit changes, gradient norms and cap status. Random-weight magnitude matching alone does not match directions or risk effects; normalization makes positive teacher scale irrelevant in exact arithmetic. Report every seed and all paired cases, not the best seed.
3. **Independent target, not self-score.** Report pre-update held-shot order accuracy, and separately same-pair post-update margin as the analytically expected check only. The decisive outcome is query **risk** `ΔBCE` after the unchanged risk head, compared on exactly the same 946 archived source VAL episodes; first average within shot and then within safe, danger and early groups. Carry through `no_update`, reverse, matched-other, shuffled and all fixed random teachers. Retain no-valid-token cases as shared no-update cases, and report known versus unknown interval origins and per-shot signs. Before the run, the main agent can adopt the existing proposal's `1e-6` safe/danger/early and balanced gates plus a requirement that true beat **each** predeclared random teacher by `1e-6` in the balanced mean. If any required group is absent or a required comparison fails, this candidate does not establish the proposed event-selected advantage; no random-seed, threshold, step, mask or scale rescue on VAL. This added random-control rule is a **recommendation**, not an already registered gate, a significance test, or a guarantee of generalization.

Interpretation: matched-other performing comparably directly challenges the added value of interval selection; random or shuffled performing comparably challenges attribution to the learned event-selected teacher. Failure against a particular control rejects this candidate/claim, not every possible temporal method. Passing all controls still supplies only a selected, development-exposed source-local screen, not a demonstrated EAST benefit or evidence of physics.

The [existing census](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/EVENT_LABEL_USE_REVIEW_20261103.md:54) is a metadata-only **upper bound before masks**: the matched near-danger TRAIN pairs come from only **10 shots**, and matched VAL safe pairs from only **5 shots**. Do not substitute those pairs for the broader [946-episode, 145-shot VAL archive](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/EVENT_LABEL_USE_REVIEW_20261103.md:70). Its frozen-source query is the retrospective peak in `origin+26..origin+50`, selected with knowledge of that later window ([selection code](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/source_episodes.py:95)); it is **not** a live selection rule or an unbiased stream.
