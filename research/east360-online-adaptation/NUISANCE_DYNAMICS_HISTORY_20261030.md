# Nuisance, dynamics and the source context-vector failure

## Decision and evidence boundary

**The saved results establish a failure of this particular feedback-to-update rule, not its unique cause.** The rule's Q/K action is almost insensitive to replacing the arrived feedback with its training mean or reversing its five blocks; it also lowers later danger logits on most selected validation windows. Confidence saturation and a loss-averaging shortcut are plausible contributors. The saved arrays cannot distinguish those from unusable incremental signal, controller saturation, or a bottleneck in the representation Jacobian. In particular, they do **not** establish that the learned 64-vector itself is constant or that the underlying signals contain no additional danger information.

This is a retrospective **J-TEXT source component** screen, not an alarm or EAST result. The source classifier and risk head were frozen according to the [run record](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/context_vector_source_v1/complete.json:2>); the original run's scientific screen failed. This sidecar did not run EAST, collect raw data, replay a model, measure new source contexts/gradients, train, or use a GPU. The older EAST results below are **previously saved development experiments**, not new runs or independent confirmation. The main agent owns any new source TRAIN/VAL measurements.

## What the saved source result actually says

The controller sees detached first-block CLS and last-token states plus five ordered blocks of masked, origin-anchored changes and availability. It maps the 358 inputs through `LayerNorm–358→32–SiLU–64–tanh`. With the representation target held fixed for the inner derivative, the fresh Q/K step is proportional to `J_origin^T v` and clipped to radius 0.1; later scores use the original risk head. [Context and step](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/learning.py:16>); [arrived-signal construction](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/observation.py:9>).

All 946 recorded real action norms are approximately 0.1. The real danger logit decreased in 105/117 windows, with per-shot mean change −0.298123. The 59 worse are **shots whose selected-window mean BCE worsened**, not 59 newly missed alarms. Real is numerically slightly better than reverse/mean on danger, but still worse than no update; safe real is worse than mean feedback. Reversing changes masks as well as blocks; controls match the real action norm, and the risk-axis control also inherits the real action's sign and norm. They are not fully independent no-feedback policies. Two observations put “task saturation/average loss” on the table, but do not prove it:

1. **Saved-VAL confidence, not a new gradient measurement.** Recomputed from the archived logits and labels, 531/638 safe windows have baseline `p<0.05`, and 66/117 danger windows have baseline `p>0.95`; median danger-window `p=0.9605`. Within the window-weighted danger sample, mean ΔBCE is +0.00821 on the 66 high-confidence points versus +0.29755 on the other 51. These are correlated windows, not independent shots; the latter calculation is a post-hoc breakdown, not the shot-weighted primary estimate. Confidence is **consistent with** small per-logit BCE derivatives for many already-correct examples, but gives no measured TRAIN parameter-gradient attribution. The equal-class mean of the two primary shot-level VAL ΔBCE values is +0.02785, so this checkpoint does not even improve that particular VAL average. [Saved arrays](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/context_vector_source_v1/validation_arrays.npz>); [record labels and order](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/context_vector_source_v1/val_records.json:1>); [shot aggregation](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/source_run.py:70>).
2. **Balanced draws are not balanced independent information.** Training drew 3,072 safe and 3,072 danger examples, with replacement and class/shot balancing. Recounting the saved index trace: all 196 danger windows from 129 shots were drawn, median 14 times per distinct danger window; only 2,609 of 9,293 safe windows were drawn, median once per distinct drawn safe window. This supports a risk of repetition and unstable generalization, not a diagnosis of overfitting or an excuse to call the batch class-imbalanced. Early disruptive windows were diagnostic only, not negative training examples. [Sampler](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/source_episodes.py:152>); [saved draws](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/context_vector_source_v1/training.json:1>); [population](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/context_vector_source_v1/complete.json:11>).

**Timing/selection limits.** Danger means a source point-positive query, with source `max(25 ms, AdvancedTime)` labeling and strict source lead >10 ms. From saved records, 85/117 VAL danger windows have lead ≤30 ms; 32/117 have lead >30 ms. TRAIN has 28/196 danger windows with lead >30 ms, from 17 shots. These figures describe selected queries only; they are not EAST eligibility, timely TPs, or independent physical precursor labels. Within each later interval the frozen parent's **future peak** selects the query, so this is an offline component diagnostic, not an online query selection policy. [Selection](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/source_episodes.py:95>); [source protocol](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/CONTEXT_VECTOR_SOURCE_PLAN_20261030.md:19>).

**What is and is not identified.** Norm equality after projection does not prove every *unprojected* action was clipped; preprojection norms are not in the validation arrays. A near-unity same-origin Q/K cosine could arise from an almost constant target, changes to `v` in the null space of `J_origin^T`, a dominant controller/context component, or geometry/projection. It does not imply the Q/K vectors are globally parallel across different origins. Nor does a fixed full-context control mean an identical Q/K action across origins: the origin Jacobian still varies. The recorded real action is not simply the origin risk axis: on danger windows its median cosine with the origin-risk gradient is −0.412 and 99/117 are negative, but that still does not identify the feature or optimization cause. The saved NPZ has logits, norms and cosines, not the contexts, vectors, preprojection steps or per-example TRAIN gradients. [Array-producing evaluation](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/prepare.py:76>).

The pre-existing CPU audit reports a shot-bootstrap descriptive interval for danger real−no-update of [+0.0493, +0.1425]; it does not include method-selection or seed uncertainty. The original run read no TEST/EAST **signals**, but the existing audit notes that the full AdvancedTime metadata mapping, including TEST entries, was parsed before role filtering and lacked a pre-run hash. Do not describe this as complete TEST-metadata isolation. [Audit](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/context_vector_source_v1/audit_cpu_v1.json:59>); [provenance limitations](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/docs/research/source_event_rebuild_20261023/炮内表示目标学习_冷启动教学与更新能力诊断_20261030.md:264>).

## Falsifiable discriminators and genuinely different candidates

These are **proposals only**, conditional on separate authorization and the main agent's measurements. “Not identified as a completed exact combination in the materials reviewed” is not a repository-wide absence search or a novelty claim. No proposed action is an instruction to run it in this sidecar.

1. **Resolve information versus utilization before another adapter.** Interpret the main agent's planned source measurements with whole-shot separation and the same fixed parent: compare a prefix/mask-only predictor with an equal-capacity prefix+actually matured-arrival predictor on the same later-query label and same evaluation rows. Controls should hold the origin, availability pattern and per-shot weighting fixed while replacing arrival by training-only mean, suitable within-mask reversal, and cross-shot mismatches; include a control for prefix refitting. Require a predeclared paired, class-specific held-shot log-loss advantage over these controls, not merely better training loss, generic order accuracy, or a more variable vector. Report results by baseline confidence and by the sparse source >30 ms subset, with shot counts and uncertainty. If arrival helps held-shot prediction but the controller/action does not change with arrival, information is available to this probe but not effectively used by this rule; if it changes the vector but not `Jᵀv`, the bottleneck may be the inner update geometry. If neither prefix+arrival nor an appropriate richer causal representation beats controls, the current experiment has **not demonstrated** incremental information; limited sampling/power or a bad probe prevent a proof of absence. The later risk window can itself contain arrived samples, so also assess incremental usefulness relative to that same later input offline; do not feed future labels or future inputs to the online rule. Prior five-point linear/neural arrival probes and scalar H25 conditional-likelihood Q/K updates have already failed specific screens. [Earlier probes and controls](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/ARRIVAL_EVIDENCE_PRIOR_ART_20261030.md:7>).
2. **Two-timescale internal state, with danger-preserving separation.** One distinct candidate is a causal, shot-reset **slow background state updated across successive matured observations**, plus a separately retained signed fast innovation that drives a bounded internal adapter; leave the original source input, absolute level and risk head intact. A slow state must be based only on previously arrived values/masks, with explicit unsupported behavior and a predeclared robust update rule; no current outcome, disruption time or unverified “normal” label may gate it. This differs from resetting a local 128-point ridge/mean fit, the fixed source expected-forecast residual, and the old one-window gain adapter; the exact combined implementation was not established in this bounded review. Compare off, fixed background, slow-background-only without neural update, raw-arrival adapter, matched-norm mean/reversed/mismatched arrival, and real dual-state adaptation. Reject if real residuals do not beat these controls on held-shot safe **and** timely-danger outcomes, or if slow adaptation absorbs gradual/sustained precursors or amplifies benign drift. Preserve level and innovation in parallel; subtraction alone is not evidence that the removed part is nuisance, and predictive fit alone is not alarm benefit.
3. **Escape the first-CLS Jacobian image, rather than only changing a target loss.** A different candidate is a small context-conditioned generator of bounded low-rank temporary Q/K matrix updates, with both factors (or their mixture) depending on genuinely matured observations, evaluated through the frozen base and original risk head. This is not the present `J_origin^T v` constrained image, the old single shared ridge gradient transport, a fixed-basis gradient projection, or scalar origin-risk-gradient feedback. Those adjacent mechanisms were tried; no claim is made that conditional low-rank adaptation is generally new. At each identical origin compare real vs mean/reversed/mispaired context, a fixed-basis/fixed-history generator and the present vector rule, matching action norm **and reporting** origin-logit and later-logit effect; a matched norm alone does not match function-space impact. Require reproducibly non-collinear, context-sensitive directions **and** subsequent paired safe/danger improvements; a different direction alone is not success. A retrospective label-informed reachability check, if separately authorized, is only an upper/feasibility diagnostic for its tested parameter family, never a deployable update or proof of a global optimum. [Prior transport and controls](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/NEXT_DIRECTION_HISTORY_AUDIT_20261030.md:15>); [old learned Q/K basis](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/docs/research/source_event_rebuild_20261023/历史反馈权重与注意力更新方向_配对实验_20261029.md:60>).

Only a later separately approved EAST evaluation could establish prequential value: freeze the qualified source and original risk head, compare the same cold parent, input contract, sequence and fixed threshold, use matured observations only, and count first alarms with strict lead >30 ms, FPR ≤0.20, all rescues and harms, and >3000 ms alarms separately. The reused development 360 and cold20 with only three safe shots are not independent confirmation. None of the candidate mechanisms is established to pass this gate.
