# EAST input-alignment history and decision — 2026-10-31

## Decision

**There is a real, completed frozen-source target→source covariance control, but it is not the proposed SAFE→SAFE, multi-feature/full-input test.** In the historical first-10 run, the target prefix happened to be **0 disruptive / 10 safe** under the HDF5-authoritative labels. Selection was blind to outcome, however, and source reference statistics pooled **all** J-TEXT TRAIN shots. Its covariance maps were separate **4×4 SXR and 4×4 XUV blocks**, on top of a density map, not a joint covariance across the other inputs. The same accidental all-safe target prefix was also used by the all-feature diagonal warmstart controls. See the [prefix class audit](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/experiments/risk_aligned_online/runs/warmstart_alignment_test360_v2_20261022/verification.json:8), [blind-selection contract](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/experiments/risk_aligned_online/runs/warmstart_alignment_test360_v2_20261022/protocol.json:414), and [covariance block definitions](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/covariance_alignment_20261022/alignment.py:13).

In the bounded material inspected, I found **no completed, explicitly outcome-selected SAFE-source and SAFE-target frozen-classifier diagonal or joint full-feature covariance map**. This is not an exhaustive claim about other worktrees, deleted/remote artifacts, or every archived experiment. The existing 10-safe-target controls are relevant negative evidence and must not be described as an entirely untried safe-target case; they do not settle the new SAFE→SAFE question.

Deep CORAL's natural-window source alignment sampler had **1,317 eligible source shots: 153D/1,164S**, versus all 20 target prefix shots (2D/18S); the source supervised sampler retained the full TRAIN pool. - A new SAFE→SAFE map on this order would be a **proposed experiment, not completed evidence**. To attribute its effect, keep the parent weights, threshold, masking contract, prefix predictions and ordering fixed; fit only from source TRAIN and already-ended target shots under an explicit label authority, record actual per-channel/block coverage and regularization, and evaluate paired full360 **and** common suffix. Compare pooled and SAFE-selected reference populations without claiming equal sample support; an unavailable/constant feature or underdetermined covariance must be logged rather than silently fabricated. Then test intra separately against the same mapped static model. This audit authorizes or performs no such run.
