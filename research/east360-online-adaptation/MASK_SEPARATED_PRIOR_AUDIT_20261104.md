## Integration boundaries / blockers

1. Historical LOCAL sample **model outputs, gradients, receipts and states are cold-conditioned**: its [producer explicitly loaded the old cold state](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/difference_feedback_probe_20261102/run.py:162). Reuse only the verified identities/query coordinates and permitted numerical map, not those neural results or `cold.pt`; recompute all pilot arms from the fresh source. The query selection used revealed outcomes, so it is retrospective, not a deployable online selector.
2. The new stem remains unverified here. Before interpreting its pilot, independently check preservation of the decoder's actual token layout, 23-channel ordering, source coordinates, missingness and causal maturity; match real/reverse/hold availability masks. The successful old-interface synthetic load alone does not establish this.
3. The map and these 20 shots are a known development budget. Do not retroactively present post-20 mapped results as the original first-20 sealed predictions, or use their labels before each shot's prediction is sealed. This audit does not establish online gains or independent validation.
