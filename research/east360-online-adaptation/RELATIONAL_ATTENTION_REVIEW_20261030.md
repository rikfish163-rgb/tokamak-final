## Concrete implementation and causal-input checks

- **Held-shot inputs stop at maturity.** Fixed endpoints are o=127+25k, m=o+25 and q=m+25. A fresh state is formed from the latest 128 observations through m, then evaluated at q. The input contract is pointwise, the shared mapping is a frozen pointwise affine transform, and window extraction ends inclusively at its supplied endpoint. Reversal changes only the final 25 values/masks for target construction; model input stays real. No risk-peak/outcome endpoint selection occurs. [Schedule](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/run.py:27); [collection](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/run.py:228); [window extraction](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/simple_data.py:41); [reversal](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/learning.py:151).
- **Loss/support/head indexing matches the formula.** Common availability requires all six measured channels, at least six observations per eight-point patch and two adjacent valid patches per difference. Keys satisfy 1≤j≤i−2 for queries i=12…15, with at least four keys per row. KL renormalizes head-0 Q/K attention on those exact edges; packed dimensions 0…15 and Q/K matrix output rows 0…15 are the same first head. CLS is token 16 and is excluded from the auxiliary edges, while the actual risk forward retains full attention. [Targets](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/learning.py:157); [KL](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/learning.py:210); [head step](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/learning.py:248); [adapter layout](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/attention_lowrank_20261029/model.py:31).
- **Saved masks/norms support the implementation.** All 2,707 opportunities have all four valid rows and all 46 allowable edges, totaling 124,522 edges; no opportunity is silently excluded by common support. Targets are nonnegative and zero outside support; maximum row-mass error is 2.39e-7. All five adaptive arms have 2,707 nonzero steps, with norm deviations from 0.05 below 3e-9. The present data do not exercise the unsupported branch.
- **CV applies to the component references/controls, not the parent.** Fold artifacts exclude held IDs, cover every shot once, and hold out 1 safe plus 6/6/5 disruptive shots. References use fit-shot first256 points, equal-shot ridge with unpenalized intercept and slope penalty1. Fixed target and raw mean-gradient controls use fit shots only. Their references are finite, rank3 is recorded for each fold, D respects its 1e-4 floor, and saved mean gradients are exactly zero outside head0. The original input mapping's calibration IDs are all20, and the frozen parent's reuse is explicitly declared. [Reference fit](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/learning.py:47); [fixed controls](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/run.py:118); [fold isolation](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/run.py:247).
- **Risk labels do not define the relation target.** Outcomes are read before predictions to stratify the known, ended cold20 shots; grouping happens after each prediction seal. They do not enter schedule, normalized signal targets, KL or fixed controls. The saved risk cosine uses the maturity-time cold logit gradient, not a label-derived BCE gradient or future query gradient. First-head direct-signal KL is intentionally unrelated to risk supervision; failure to improve risk is an experimental finding, not evidence that the code omitted a required risk loss. [Outcomes/folds](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/run.py:239); [gradient diagnostics](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/relational_attention_20261030/run.py:102).
