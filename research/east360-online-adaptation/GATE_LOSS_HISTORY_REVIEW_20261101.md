# Bounded history review: forecast-loss changes for the 32-gate action space

## Decision and duplication warning

**Do not describe centered or differenced forecasting, channel normalization, or training a feedback proxy against later risk as new ideas in this project.** Each has an executed close precedent below. The strongest combined precedent is `internal_dynamics_M`: a separately trained, change-normalized H25 forecaster drives a multi-token temporary update, and source future risk is differentiated through those updates. Its complete EAST360 result was worse than its own static reference. The closest **ended-EAST20, fixed-parent gradient-alignment** precedent is `operational_transport_cold20_v2` followed by `operational_transport_east360_v1`: its fitted mapping and its real/reverse full-stream arms produced no operational gain.

**What remains untested in this bounded review** is narrower: the exact existing **16×2 short/long/snapshot token-gate action space**, frozen original absolute-asinh H25 head and frozen parent, with the original loss's *output gradient* separated into per-channel time-constant DC and zero-mean AC components, and their consequences checked against later-risk directions using only the 20 ended target shots. I found no completed experiment in the specified histories and their inspected references establishing that exact combination. This is a scoped absence finding, **not** a repository-wide novelty claim and not evidence of benefit. In particular, a future loss change should be described as an action-space-specific extension of earlier centering/difference work, not as the first such method.

Comparisons below are within each historical run only. The qualified source is fixed, online adaptation is EAST-only, and HDF5 `IsDisrupt`/`DownTime` remain the authority for outcomes ([reader](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/io23.py:99)). Old runs have different parent states, input maps, orders, thresholds, or feedback clocks. They cannot be numerically subtracted from the present 125/35 baseline. All described runs are development evidence, not independent future confirmation.

## Present gate baseline and what the high cosine does—and does not—show

The saved [dual-history protocol](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/dual_history_ttt_v1/protocol.json) and [completion](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/dual_history_ttt_v1/completed.json) identify a complete 360-shot run, source unchanged, warmup20 included and no persistent neural updates. The 16 token pairs satisfy nonnegative per-pair weights summing to at most one; they mix two frozen token differences, long-minus-short and snapshot-minus-short, before the frozen second block. The snapshot repeats the last observed values rather than providing a second real history. Before endpoint 254 the long view falls back to the short view, so its difference vanishes; the nominal 32 coordinates need not all have an effect at every clock. The original forecast head emits 25×23 absolute-asinh predictions ([model](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/dual_history_ttt_20261101/model.py:142)).

On that run, full360 TP/FP were cold **125/35**, real **125/35**, reverse **125/35**, hold **126/35**, and the fixed `long_data` view **126/31**. `long_data` is a static view intervention, not substantive within-shot learning. Real made **10,910** nonzero-gradient proposals, of which **10,895** were accepted; the saved operational and control-separation gates failed. The saved [gate diagnostic](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/dual_history_ttt_v1/gate_diagnostics.json) gives real/reverse gradient cosine median **0.9996587**. This is an event-weighted descriptive statistic from saved receipts, not 340 independent observations: the [diagnostic code](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/dual_history_ttt_20261101/diagnose.py:49) compares each arm's gradients along its own evolving gate trajectory. The saved gradients include the **0.01 mean-square gate anchor**, not just the forecast component. A high cosine does not by itself establish DC domination, Huber saturation, or a causal reason for no alarm gain.

The actual [loss](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/simple23.py:142) is masked, mean-reduced SmoothL1 on predictions against absolute-asinh targets, with default β=1. The [stream](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/dual_history_ttt_20261101/stream.py:6) uses only fully matured H25 values, excludes the discrete channel and intersects real/reversed support; its first scheduled update is at endpoint 159, first possible effect at 160, then every 128. The anchored forecast-objective gradient is normalized to make a proposed step; the resulting gate values are projected to the simplex and retained only if the **original anchored objective** strictly decreases ([step](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/dual_history_ttt_20261101/model.py:208)). The current `model.py`, `stream.py`, and `simple23.py` SHA-256 values match their respective entries in the saved protocol; this check is not a rerun.

## Closest executed predecessors

### 1. Relative and channel-normalized SmoothL1 plus multi-token future-risk teaching

The old [internal dynamics implementation](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/internal_dynamics_20261022/model.py:84) uses

`target[h,c] = (asinh_future[h,c] − asinh_origin[c]) / source_change_scale[c]`

with masked SmoothL1 and excludes the discrete mode. Source change scales are per-channel RMS of available changes at horizons 1, 12 and 25 ([preparation](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/internal_dynamics_20261022/prepare.py:27)). A separately trained decoder predicts those normalized changes; a temporary 8×64 matrix changes valid data tokens between frozen Transformer blocks, not CLS directly. The `M` source preparation differentiates later source-risk BCE through three arrived forecast steps and adds 0.2 forecast loss ([objective](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/internal_dynamics_20261022/prepare.py:101)).

Its saved old EAST360 [M completion](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/experiments/risk_aligned_online/runs/internal_EAST_M_20261022/completed.json) records **141 TP/92 FP** and 59,761 intra updates, versus its own [static](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/experiments/risk_aligned_online/runs/internal_EAST_static_20261022/completed.json) **145/94**. J falls from 0.2833 to 0.2722. This is a failed joint alarm trade-off, not a proof that all relative targets or all multi-token actions fail. It used an old unmapped parent, a learned decoder/basis and a 512-coordinate fast matrix; it did **not** split the original absolute forecast-head gradient in the present 32-gate geometry. The [prior review](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/META_ADAPT_HISTORY_REVIEW_20261101.md:20) documents its archived-stream provenance; this review did not rerun it.

### 2. Explicit removal of older per-channel H25 residual bias

The [lag-bias code](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/lag_bias_feedback_20261022/learning.py:23) estimates a detached mean `prediction − truth` per channel on two older completed H25 segments, subtracts it from residuals on two later segments, then applies SmoothL1 to update a temporary encoder. The older and newer targets are disjoint. This is the closest executed **forecast-residual bias subtraction**, but it centers a *residual using older segments before applying SmoothL1*; it is not the DC/AC decomposition of the current loss's clipped output gradient. It updates 16 encoder tensors, not 32 view gates, and does not center the risk input.

The [historical audit](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/INTRA_FORECAST_HISTORY_20261031.md:74) and [saved completion](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/experiments/risk_aligned_online/runs/lag_bias_feedback_test360_20261022/completed.json) report old TEST360 static **118/27**, matched recent-uncorrected **117/29**, and lag-bias **119/27**. The one extra TP versus static crossed the strict 30 ms lead boundary by only 1 ms; the reported advantage disappears at strict >31 ms. This is a fragile positive, **not** a blanket failure. It used a different initial20, map and parent. Do not rebrand “older residual mean → detach → correct newer H25” as a new idea or infer that sustained residuals are necessarily nuisance.

### 3. Centered latent fast-window learning and normalized update

The [anchored fast-window code](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/anchored_fast_window_20261023/model.py:6) centers observed latent keys and values across valid tokens, then takes a window-local linear regression update; its `normalized` arm divides by a curvature-trace term. It applies a fast residual across valid tokens, including valid CLS. This is **not** normalization of the original H25 forecast error and not the current gate-simplex action.

The saved [complete360](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/experiments/risk_aligned_online/runs/anchored_fast_window360_20261023/completed.json) gives parent **132/37**, centered fixed-step **117/28**, and centered normalized **116/30**, at its own threshold 0.1254041493. Both lose substantial detections; normalized reverse is **180/180**. It is a warning against calling centered, normalized multi-token fast learning untried, not an exact test of the new gradient decomposition.

### 4. Ended-target20 teaching from forecast gradients to future operational gradients

The closest **same conceptual supervision** is [operational transport preparation](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/operational_transport_20261030/prepare.py:92). It fits a class/shot-weighted, centered ridge map of independently unit-normalized gradient rows, λ=0.1 ([fit](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/operational_transport_20261030/learning.py:36)). The map, rather than the original forecast head, is the trained proxy; online it maps the arrived forecast gradient into a fresh, bounded Q/K state. Ended20 outcomes supervise preparation, not the current shot's update.

The [cold20 receipt](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/operational_transport_cold20_v2/complete.json) reports 688 examples, 20 shots and zero source updates; the [protocol](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/operational_transport_cold20_v2/protocol.json) records that the parent had already seen all20 and no suffix labels entered fitting. ## Practical guardrails for the already-scoped DC/AC check

These are distinctions for the current offline20 diagnostic, not proposals to train a new generic slope or meta-controller:

1. **Decompose the differentiated loss, not merely the target or raw residual.** For observed horizon entries of channel `c`, let `z=asinh((target−source_center)/source_scale)`, `r=prediction−z` and `u=clip(r,−1,1)/N`, where `N` is the total observed-scalar denominator used by the original loss. For each shot/example and channel, compute the mean of `u` over its valid horizon entries, broadcast that mean only to valid entries as `u_DC`, and let `u_AC=u−u_DC`; an unsupported channel contributes zero. Use the same masks, model state, predictions and gate Jacobian for both components. Then `g_forecast=Jᵀu_DC + Jᵀu_AC`; add the anchor gradient separately before checking agreement with the saved total. One valid horizon entry has zero AC by construction. Centering residuals *before* SmoothL1 instead yields a different object, particularly in the saturated regime. Likewise, projecting an already clipped output gradient is not automatically the gradient of a scalar centered SmoothL1 loss.
2. **Keep output-space and gate-space geometry distinct.** DC and AC are orthogonal in the observed output space under this projection, but `Jᵀu_DC` and `Jᵀu_AC` need not be orthogonal in 32-dimensional gate space. Normalization, the nonnegative simplex projection, the anchor, and acceptance against the original objective can all change the direction or suppress movement. Compare real/reverse components at the **same** gate state as well as along their original separate trajectories; record zero/unsupported cases and whether the β=1 Huber threshold is crossed. The existing 0.9996587 cosine alone does not locate the cause.
3. **Only ended20 may supply retrospective risk direction for this scope.** Compare the actual proposed/projected gate displacement with a later-risk-loss gradient of the same fixed parent and state, with sign and timing stated; an unprojected cosine is not an alarm outcome. Distinguish later-risk targets, threshold/lead definitions, class/shot weighting and real/reverse/hold support. The 20-shot set has 17 disruptive and only 3 safe shots in the cited conditional-parent histories; folds cannot make the shared parent/map independent, and 688 windows are not 688 independent shots. No online update may use the current shot's outcome or future samples. HDF5 outcomes remain authoritative when outcome access is authorized.
4. **Do not promote on auxiliary loss or static-view counts.** A forecast loss decrease, a distinct gradient, or `long_data` 126/31 is not evidence that causal within-shot gate learning improves alarm decisions. Any later full-stream claim needs its own authorized, paired, sealed comparison against the exact 125/35 parent and real/reverse/hold controls, with current-shot outcomes unavailable until prediction sealing; that future experiment is outside this review.
