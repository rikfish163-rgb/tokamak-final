# Mask-separated EAST20 pilot: independent bounded audit

**Conclusion: the candidate gate fails.** On these retrospectively selected development episodes, `reference_bt/real` improves the safe-shot margin loss but worsens disruptive-shot and 30–125 ms loss; its balanced delta is also worse than both same-parent controls. This is a local diagnostic, not a measured operational alarm gain, an efficacy claim, or evidence of a physical law.

Inputs: the [plan](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/MASK_SEPARATED_INTRA_PLAN_20261104.md), [pilot artifacts](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/mask_separated_ended20_20261104_v1), [fresh outcome metadata](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/probe_outputs/hdf5_outcome_authority_20261104_v1), and the hash-matched [runner](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/mask_separated_intra_20261103/run.py). This audit used CPU calculations only; it did not read raw signal arrays, train, run the GPU, or replay the source model. It reread only the 20 HDF5 files' outcome fields and `data/ip` clock attributes/dataset shapes.

## Construction and evidentiary boundaries

The inspected runner loads the hash-locked qualified source, creates fresh zero-slow adapters, and strictly loads the source `forecast_only` head; the inspected execution path has no `cold.pt` or target-fitted-head restore. It constructs the origin pack through `q−26`, trains its temporary 4×64 state on the matured `q−25…q−1` diagnostics and shared symmetric availability mask, and evaluates at `q`. Real, reversed, and held target controls use the same observed support. It patches `io23.read_outcome` to raise during the neural block, writes predictions/states and the seal before calling the post-seal outcome reader, and checks frozen-state digests. These are **code-path and recorded-process checks**, not an independently observed runtime trace or proof against all possible uninstrumented access.

There is an important streaming limitation: `io23.read_signals` reads whole-shot arrays before the runner slices prefixes; the pointwise input transform also validates the whole loaded shot. Thus the inspected neural/update inputs are prefix-sliced, but the run is **not** a literal arrived-data-only I/O implementation, and future-dependent validation can affect whether a shot completes. The outcome-informed coordinate selection and a numerical map prepared from these same 20 ended shots are retrospective development resources, not target-blind online selection. The map was fit from the ended-shot records and can encode observations later than a particular query; no claim is made that this occurred for a specific sample. Each episode resets state to zero, so this is not a persistent full-shot trajectory.

The mask shift algebra moves the CIII/MNM mask-column contributions onto the Bt mask column without changing the actual availability mask or original weights. It is exactly zero when those three mask indicators agree, including common padding; that source-pattern equivalence does **not** establish correctness or improvement for target-specific missingness. The auxiliary-loss acceptance, parity witnesses, and hash checks do not establish successful warning performance. The audit does not independently attest the model/reasoning allocation; service-tier control was unavailable.
