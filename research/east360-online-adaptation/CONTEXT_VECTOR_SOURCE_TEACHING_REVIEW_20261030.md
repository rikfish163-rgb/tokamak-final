# Source teaching for the current context-vector target — 2026-10-30

## Bottom line

**Yes, it is a distinct, bounded within-project experiment, not a new general idea or evidence of benefit.** I did not find source TRAIN1353 teaching of *this* `ContextTarget` and its actual representation-induced Q/K step in the searched code/history. What is already established is broader: source-only teaching of an adaptation rule, event-auxiliary teaching, task-aligned forecast heads through the actual Q/K update, and source-trained representation completion have all been tried. The proposed change is **which inner update is taught**, plus the transfer from source to this cold20 controller—not the mere use of source data, meta-gradients, an auxiliary head, or a representation loss. This is a bounded search, not proof of absence throughout project history or a literature-novelty claim. [History audit](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/NEXT_DIRECTION_HISTORY_AUDIT_20261030.md:11>).

The current rule has 14,316 parameters including LayerNorm: detached first-block CLS and last-token representations (128), plus five chronological blocks of 23 masked asinh-relative means and 23 availability fractions (230), feed LayerNorm–358→32 SiLU→64 tanh. For a zero Q/K state and same-forward origin CLS `h`, it differentiates `0.5 ||h - stopgrad(h) - v(context)||²` and takes a fresh `eta=0.1` step clipped to radius `0.1` in 8,192 first-layer Q/K coordinates. Before clipping this is `0.1 J_origin^T v`; the unchanged risk head evaluates later inputs. The target is not added to a logit. The update is confined to the at-most-64-dimensional origin-CLS Jacobian image; larger source training does not remove this limitation. [Controller and step](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/learning.py:16>); [Q/K and readout](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/attention_lowrank_20261029/model.py:31>).

## Feasibility and contracts

- **Source labels and coverage are not EAST labels.** Canonical source roles are disjoint TRAIN1353/VAL160/TEST220; J-TEXT point labels use `horizon=max(25 ms, AdvancedTime)`, with missing AdvancedTime falling back to 25 ms. The old matched Q/K screen used source positive `lead>10 ms`; EAST's current timely query is `30 < lead <= 125 ms`. [Source contract](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/io23.py:110>); [EAST screen](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/context_vector_20261030/prepare.py:190>). A read-only join of the [canonical TRAIN list](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/artifacts/canonical/jtext_source_bundle_p131_v1/index.json:17814>) with [AdvancedTime](</media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison/shot_list/J-TEXT/AdvancedTime_J-TEXT.json:1>) gives 1,166 missing and only **30/1,353** values above 30 ms; none lies in `(29.999999, 30]`, the range that could matter for the label function's 1e-6 ms tolerance. These 30 include any safe shots and have not been checked for valid matured queries, so they are only an **upper bound** on disruptive TRAIN shots with a source-positive point at lead >30 ms. Do not silently impose EAST's cutoff on the source point-positive population or extend source labels to 125 ms. In the different old `query>=256` experiment, 102 TRAIN shots were excluded; eligible groups were 1,162 safe, 62 early-disruptive and 83 danger, with overlap between the latter groups. This does not establish coverage under the proposed 25-point clock. Intervals are `[start,end)` on the absolute clock; unannotated is −1/unknown, not safe. The labels came from whole-record offline analysis and have historical split exposure; they are neither certified causal physics nor current EAST phase labels. ## One smallest discriminating test — proposal only

After the current run is sealed and only if separately authorized, test **one raw/fixed-radius source-initialization hypothesis**, not a hyperparameter/event sweep. No new experiment is run in this review.

1. Freeze the qualified source checkpoint, scaler, original risk head/threshold, EAST cold20 map and `slow`. Offline only, create TRAIN episodes using the exact current 358-vector and fresh Q/K representation step (`eta=0.1`, radius 0.1), with origins `127+25k`, maturity `o+25`, and queries in `o+26..o+50`. Use a predeclared, frozen-parent peak query selection analogous to cold20, but source-valid, strict **source** `lead>10 ms` and its existing point-positive horizon. Use later-query raw-logit BCE as the outer loss, differentiating through the actual representation step. Train on safe and source timely-positive queries only, balancing class then shot then windows; do not turn early disruptive windows into safe examples. Keep early windows for logit-drift diagnostics. Enumerate and report eligible shots/windows, missing masks and exclusions **before fitting**. Train only `ContextTarget`, with 512 fixed Adam steps, batch 12 (six per class), seed 131, lr 0.001, clip 1, final checkpoint only. Source `slow=None`; no event head or source-label input to the inner step. This reuses the old matched-head step/batch budget but changes its objective/site; it is not a reproduction of that experiment.
2. Freeze that checkpoint. On disjoint source VAL160, use fixed, predeclared origin/query sampling and report per-shot mean ΔBCE separately for safe and source timely-positive shots; compare real feedback to no update, five-block reverse, and training-only mean feedback. Norm-match reverse and mean to each real action; no update remains zero and zero control directions are never fabricated. Report early-disruptive logit drift, zero support, actual action norms and same-origin direction cosines. Source VAL is developmental and the frozen parent was previously qualified, so this is not independent confirmation. Do not use VAL to choose checkpoints or change the label horizon.
3. If the source screen is adequately populated and passes, use the **same three existing cold20 folds, same raw contexts/176 eligible rows, exact frozen conditional parent/map/slow, same 30 epochs, seed, minibatch order and fresh Adam state** for two initializations: existing zero-init EAST-only versus source-pretrained then EAST-fold-fit. Pair results by held shot on the archived queries, keep original action norms for the primary comparison and report a separate common-norm direction comparison (zeros remain zero). This isolates the practical contribution of *adding source initialization* under the fixed recipe, not whether it is due to sample count, domain content, or training compute.
