# Arrival-conditional evidence: scoped prior art and protocol critique

## Verdict

The **idea of a weighted logistic arrival increment and the `p − r` temporary-Q/K mechanism have both been run locally**, but in separate experiments. Within the selected implementations and indexed reports inspected here, I found **no completed run combining independently fitted `q0(y|prefix)` and `q1(y|prefix, H25 arrival)` with their logit difference as feedback to temporary Q/K**. This is a scoped finding, not a claim of repository-wide or literature-wide novelty.

## Protocol risks and discriminating checks

- **Subtracting logits:** the difference has a conditional log density ratio interpretation when `q0` and `q1` are correct conditionals of the **same weighted joint distribution**, with common support and the same origin label/rows. Strong ridge and tiny, correlated shot samples can mis-specify them differently; marginal calibration of each score alone is not sufficient. Call it an *estimated evidence score* until shot-held diagnostics support the ratio interpretation. Classifier-to-ratio methods and their calibration assumptions are discussed by [Cranmer, Pavez and Louppe, §§2.2–2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/1506.02169v2); that paper does not validate this experiment.
- **Weighting:** the old weighting allocates half the mass to each stage label, then equally among shot IDs within each label, then among that shot-label's windows. A disruptive shot can contribute to both labels. Identical row weights make both fits target the same weighted empirical joint, but shot-dependent weighting may alter class-conditional feature distributions; it is **not generally just a removable class-prior intercept**. Preserve paired rows, mask policy and fold-only normalization, and report effective shots/positive windows per fold. [Implementation](/media/hetaisheng/044A81D94A81C83E/Fuison-intra-trajectory-20261028/experiments/risk_aligned_online/conditional_forecast_20261023/learning.py:10).
- **Prefix leakage into the difference:** independently refitted linear logits yield `(a1−a0)+(b1−b0)ᵀprefix+cᵀarrival`; thus even zero arrival can give nonzero “evidence” from changed intercept/prefix coefficients. Check matched no-arrival/constant, earlier and mispaired controls; the old offset model deliberately made its zero-change increment zero. A synthetic zero-arrival pattern may itself be outside training support. Avoid interpreting a purely prefix-driven difference as newly arrived information.
- **Origin target versus later risk:** `y` for fitting describes the **origin's** 125 ms state; later +26…+50 labels describe a different time. A negative origin can precede a positive later point. Seal the origin score before arrival, construct the update only from arrived H25, report origin/arrival eligibility and future-time timely `lead>30 ms` separately, and never use the current outcome or later labels in the feedback. An improvement on one target is not automatically an improvement on the other.
- **Frozen-source posterior and double counting:** adding the estimated increment to `z_origin` is a Bayesian update only to the extent that the frozen risk is a compatible prior for the *same origin event, population and prefix information*. If the risk is instead recomputed using the arrived H25, that information may be counted twice; if it is miscalibrated or conditions on less/more than `q0`, the expression is a feedback pseudo-target, not a validated posterior. Same weights for `q0/q1` do not calibrate the frozen source model. Later input windows may also contain H25 information already used for the Q/K update; compare the same later inputs with and without the temporary state rather than claiming independent evidence.
- **Missingness:** decide whether the evidence is for the joint arrival `(values, mask)` or for values conditional on the mask. The latter also needs a prior conditioned on that mask, unless an explicit noninformative-missingness assumption holds. Zero-filling alone can encode absence as a signal; inspect availability and support by shot/class, and define an explicit no-supported-observation behavior without treating it as proven Bayes evidence.
- **Cold20 uncertainty:** only 3/20 shots are safe, so at least two of five disjoint 4-held folds contain no safe shot. Report per-shot held predictions and pooled shot-level class summaries without treating absent fold-class metrics as zero; do not tune on held folds or infer deployment FPR from three safe shots.
- **What would distinguish the proposed mechanism:** verify that only temporary Q/K parameters change after H25 arrives, that the risk head/source and earlier predictions remain fixed, and that +26…+50 scores are recomputed by the original risk network with those changed parameters. Record nonzero Q/K state, `p−r`, direction and subsequent score changes alongside no-update, no-information and time/mispair controls. A `q1` BCE improvement alone is an auxiliary-classifier result; directly outputting `r` or adding the increment to the output is a scalar score correction, not the requested Q/K feedback. Any screen remains development evidence, not a 360-shot alarm claim.
