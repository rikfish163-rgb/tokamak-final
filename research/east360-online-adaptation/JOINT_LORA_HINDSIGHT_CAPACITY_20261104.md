# Precommitted hindsight capacity diagnostic

Status: design fixed before selecting cases or reading raw signal arrays. This is a deliberately optimistic within-shot hindsight oracle, not a causal method, an online result, a deployment candidate, or a mathematical upper bound. The fixed development stream is not independent validation.

## Frozen design

- Case selection uses only the sealed qualified-source static360 `map_after20` predictions and the v5 HDF5 metadata inventory. Exclude the first 20 locked stream entries. Verify the completion artifacts and per-shot seals before using them. For disrupted shots require HDF `IsDisrupt=1`, no valid preterminal source alarm, and at least one index at or after 135 with rounded lead strictly greater than 30 ms and at most 3,000 ms. The margin is the unchanged probability threshold minus the maximum sealed probability over that eligible band. Select the first two stream-order cases with margin in `(0, 0.1]` (close), and the first two with margin at least `0.4` (deep). For safe shots require HDF `IsDisrupt=0`, first source false alarm at index at least 151, four support positions from index 135 strictly before that alarm, and at least 48 samples after the alarm; select the first two in stream order. Do not substitute other cases based on model behavior. Require at least one case per stratum; use the available cases if fewer than two qualify.
- Per case, take exactly eight deterministic current-shot support endpoints. For disruptions, four are the 1/5, 2/5, 3/5 and 4/5 positions of the sorted negative endpoints (index ≥135 and lead >125 ms); four are those positions within the positive, timely endpoints (30 < lead ≤125 ms). For safe shots, four are those positions between index 135 and the first false alarm, exclusive; the other four are the first alarm and offsets +16, +32, +48. If a chosen case cannot supply eight distinct endpoints, the design gate fails; do not change the case or window rule.
- Labels are the true hindsight HDF outcome and fixed EAST 125-ms risk window. Every shot starts from the identical risk-neutral qualified source, with last-layer rank-2 Q/V LoRA (512 trainable scalars, alpha/r=1) using the existing deterministic initialization; source risk head, map and all other parameters are fixed. Fit the eight endpoints together with 60 projected Adam steps, learning rate 0.01, no weight decay, gradient clipping 1, and joint Euclidean parameter displacement radius 0.5 from the initial LoRA parameters. One source view only. No matched non-Q/V control is required or planned under the four-minute budget.
- The adapted state is applied counterfactually from index 135, and indices 0–134 are copied exactly from the sealed source. Evaluate the complete probability timeline at unchanged threshold `0.7258946895599365`, and separately score the same timeline with the eight support endpoints replaced by the baseline values. Report held-only probability changes and margins (30–3,000 ms and 30–125 ms), full and held-spliced first-alarm TP/FP outcomes, and earlier-negative collateral including new early threshold crossings. Endpoint-disjoint does not mean temporally independent: adjacent 128-sample inputs overlap. The first-alarm splice is a diagnostic construction, not an executable alarm policy.
- Selection and all settings are recorded in a sealed `precommit.json` before any HDF signal is opened. Selected raw HDF files are checked against static360 raw SHA256 and v5 metadata before and after signal reading. Verify risk-neutral source/adapter parity against the sealed source at support and evaluation points, fixed parent state, threshold, full temporal alignment, support/held disjointness, and the unchanged prefix. Fail closed if a gate fails; do not tune to observed evaluations.
- CPU only, one computation thread; process hard timeout ≤240 s, no GPU, no edits to existing producers. Preserve all results, including failures, and describe any deviations honestly.
