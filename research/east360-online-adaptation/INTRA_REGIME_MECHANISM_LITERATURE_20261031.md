# EAST intra-shot adaptation: conditional-regime mechanism

2026-10-31. **Proposal only; no efficacy claim.** This conditional-reference extension is not a paper reproduction or novelty claim. No project data, GPU, training, inference, or current full360 results were accessed.

## Answer and identification boundary

An unlabeled arrival cannot, by itself, identify a benign shift versus a precursor. For `x = nuisance + hazard`, transferring a component between terms leaves `x` unchanged. Neither persistence nor low forecast error fixes this. A weaker question: **given the origin and availability pattern, does the ordered arrival contain information about a future disruption outcome, and can a Q/K update use it beneficially?** Predictive information is not physical causation.

This requires overlapping historical conditions, enough ended shots with event times and event-free follow-up, a stable historical-to-future relation, and a credible conditional arrival model. Indistinguishable changes or missing regime overlap remain unidentified. Shutdown may be informative censoring or a competing event. Measured current and field are not assumed exogenous actuators.

## Three primary methods and their limits

1. **Ben-David, Lu, Luu and Pál (2010), “Impossibility Theorems for Domain Adaptation.”** [PMLR full text](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v9/david10a/david10a.pdf), §3 and Definition 5; §4 Examples 6–8; §5 Theorems 1–2. With labeled source and unlabeled target examples, reweighting can help or hurt sharply in situations indistinguishable to the learner. This classification-domain result is not an EAST impossibility proof. **Transfer:** unlabeled matching does not guarantee safe adaptation; outcome stability and support are assumptions.

2. **Candès, Fan, Janson and Lv (2018), “Panning for Gold: Model-X Knockoffs for High Dimensional Controlled Variable Selection.”** [Authors' full preprint](https://lucasjanson.fas.harvard.edu/papers/Panning_For_Gold_Model_X_Knockoffs_For_High_Dimensional_Controlled_Variable_Selection-Candes_ea-2017.pdf), §4, Algorithm 2; [published-paper supplement](https://faculty.marshall.usc.edu/jinchi-lv/publications/JRSSB-CFJL18_Supplementary.pdf), §F.1, Lemma F.1 and Algorithm 1. For `H0: Y ⟂ V | C`, their conditional randomization test replaces `V` by independent draws from the **true** `P(V|C)`, while preserving `C` and `Y`; under the null the statistic is exchangeable. The stated algorithm uses independent observations. A fitted, approximate sampler has no automatic exact p-value, and this is an association test, not an adaptation algorithm or a physical-causality test. We adapt the idea to a *vector* of arrival summaries conditional also on its observed mask.

3. **Cox (1972), “Regression Models and Life-Tables.”** [Full paper](https://web.stanford.edu/~lutian/coursepdf/cox1972paper.pdf), §§2–4, Eqs. (9), (12)–(13), and §6, Eq. (21). Risk sets exclude prior failures and censoring; proportional hazards compare each failure with its risk set while leaving baseline hazard unspecified. The paper states censoring assumptions and gives a discrete-time formulation. **Transfer:** distinguish event, event-free follow-up and censored/competing termination. Our horizon-based BCE is **not** Cox partial likelihood and inherits no guarantees.

Together these motivate conditional information tests with temporal outcomes, but establish neither EAST benefit nor sampler identifiability from cold20.

## One candidate: conditional-reference anchored target

**Mechanism hypothesis.** Conditional centering may remove predictable regime-related target response while retaining arrival-specific outcome information. It does not establish that removed components are benign or residuals physical precursors. The source input and original risk pathway retain absolute levels, but centering can still suppress predictable sustained precursors.

Let `C` be the detached origin CLS plus last representation (128 values), `V` the ordered five blocks of 23 masked means of arrived asinh changes from origin, and `M` their five blocks of 23 valid fractions. At completion of the 25-point arrival, draw `V*` from a **historical, label-free** estimate `q(V|C,M)`, retaining `M`; entries with zero availability remain zero. `M` contains block fractions, not full raw masks. Use the existing 14k network `fψ` and the 64-vector

`δψ(C,V,M) = 0.5 [fψ(C,V,M) − mean_k fψ(C,V*k,M)]`.

For one concrete, intentionally restricted `q`, fit a ridge conditional Gaussian with mean linear in the first two training-shot PCA coordinates of `C` and `M`, and a rank-two-plus-diagonal residual covariance; draw eight masked vectors using fixed seeds. Fit on outcome-blind, shot-weighted origins, excluding unavailable values; fix ridge/shrinkage before evaluation. This unimodal approximation may be grossly wrong, particularly for rare masks: stop if unsupported. It cannot substitute for normal coverage.

For origin input `X_s` and temporary first-layer Q/K state `a`, use the existing loss `0.5 ||z_a(X_s) − stop(z_0(X_s)) − δψ||²` and one actual gradient step from `a=0`, with the existing joint radius. Hold `δψ` fixed with respect to `a`, but retain its outer gradient to `ψ` during historical training. Score at maturity before updating; affect **only later** predictions. Reset between shots; retain the zero-state/replacement and mask policies. No support means zero update. The J-TEXT parent, EAST mapping/slow state, risk head and alarm threshold remain fixed: no joint backbone retraining. The sampler is additional to the 14k parameters.

Train `ψ` only on eligible ended-shot episodes, minimizing shot-equal post-update outcome BCE through the actual Q/K step; fix the sampler, shot weights, horizon, budget and checkpoint rule. Train the global-anchor comparator with the same episodes, labels and target-network budget; the sampler is extra capacity. Define outcomes from recorded events within a prespecified future horizon after the query, or observed event-free follow-up through it. Treat early termination as censored or competing, **not** physically safe. The horizon must be compatible with the parent's original label/timing contract; otherwise stop. No current-shot outcome, event time, future query, or generated outcome enters the online update.

**Difference from the old result.** The prior anchor subtracts `f(C, global mean)`; its donor substitutes one nearest-origin arrival. Here the reference averages `f` under a *joint*, origin- and availability-conditional distribution: `E[f(V*)|C,M]` generally differs from `f(E[V*|C,M])`. This adjacent variant may still collapse to the failed update; previous failures stand. Class-balanced sampler fitting changes the conditional population unless weighting is explicitly intended and used consistently.

## Falsification on already-ended shots

1. **Feasibility first.** Fit sampler and origin compression on training shots only; exclude the evaluated shot. Match prospective episode and mask rules; never condition on outcome. Compression only supports a full-`C` CRT if it preserves the needed conditional law. Follow-up-based selection can also alter that law. Compare held real and generated availability-supported signed changes and serial structure by shot and operating coverage. Missing support or obvious discrepancies stop the proposal; passing does not prove `q=P(V|C,M)`.
2. **Information diagnostic, separate from operation.** Choose one origin per shot by a predeclared outcome-blind rule. Train a small historical-only diagnostic probe `g(C,M,V)` and freeze it; use held-shot `T = −mean BCE(g(C,M,V),Y)` against its conditional-draw values. This probe never changes the risk head. The null is `Y ⟂ V | (C,M)`; conditioning on `M` tests **values/order beyond availability**. Formal CRT calibration requires the true conditional distribution, independent shots and the same selection population; the estimated sampler makes this exploratory. A statistic retaining a later query containing actual `V` cannot use `q(V|C,M)` as a valid CRT: that query leaks `V`.
3. **Separate paired intervention.** On the same real later input, compare no update, global anchor, candidate real, conditional draw, reverse and donor; match clocks, masks and Q/K radius, and report action norms and later-logit effects. Draws here are policy controls, **not CRT p-values**. Prespecify shot-weighted **conditional-draw loss minus real loss** and group weights; report each normal and disruptive shot, early and timely-danger intervals, and real versus no-update loss. Do not advance the arrival-use claim if the held contrast is nonpositive, effects collapse to global anchor, or the sampler fails. Lack of power is inconclusive. Check whether sustained arrivals are progressively suppressed and early risk reduced.
4. **Operational outcome is different.** Only a later authorized, locked replay can assess first-alarm TPR/FPR, strict lead `>30 ms`, rescued/harmed shots and FPR against the existing `≤0.20` contract. Improvement in a conditional test, auxiliary loss, or mean BCE alone is not alarm benefit. A real-versus-control advantage is necessary for the *specified incremental-feedback claim*, not for every possible claim of overall utility.

## Minimum evidence and audit boundary

At least one distinct event and one observed event-free comparison are needed in each relevant fit and evaluation partition: a logical minimum, not enough for a 115-dimensional arrival model. Windows are not independent shots. Cold20 has **17 disruptive/3 normal**: with one normal held per fold, only two remain for fitting. The parent has seen all 20, so these are conditional component holdouts. The supplied prefix80 has **52 disruptive/28 normal**; if nested, its later 60 contain 35/25, subject to chronology and eligibility checks. Use only outcomes from shots ended before each evaluated shot; never use running full360 future labels.

For scale, even a prespecified representative independent sample with **zero** false alarms in three normal shots has a one-sided 95% exact upper bound of about **0.63**; 0/14 would give about **0.19**. These are hypothetical binomial calculations, not measured results, and do not survive post-selection or dependence. More representative independent normal shots and outcome-time coverage are needed for a safety claim.
